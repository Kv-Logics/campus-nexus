How Campus Relay Works

Safe, private, and peer-to-peer reverse tunneling for campus networks.

1
Connect to Campus Wi-Fi: Ensure this phone/laptop is connected to your college or university local Wi-Fi.
2
Tap "Connect as Campus Relay": A lightweight WebSocket reverse tunnel is established back to the Campus Nexus hub.
3
Keep this page open: When your friend outside campus requests an internal portal (e.g. http://10.x.x.x), this page fetches the intranet page via your Wi-Fi and securely relays the content back.
4
Safe & Isolated: No personal browsing history, passwords, or device files are ever accessed.
